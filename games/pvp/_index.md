{{% alert context="warning" %}}
**FFA HAS NO END**

This game mode is infinite and can be played with as many players as you want.
{{% /alert %}}

## 📦 Kits

FFA is played with kits. Upon your first spawn, and after each death, you will have the choice between several kits, each with its own advantages, disadvantages, and abilities.

## <img src="https://mcapi.marveldc.me/item/ironChestplate?version=1.20&#x26;width=64&#x26;height=64" alt="" data-size="line"> Fighter
The simplest kit in FFA. A melee kit with full iron armor and a QQC sword.

## <img src="https://mcapi.marveldc.me/item/bow?version=1.20&#x26;width=64&#x26;height=64" alt="" data-size="line"> Archer
Another classic and ability-less kit, this one comes with a bow and ten flaming arrows.

## <img src="https://mcapi.marveldc.me/item/diamond?version=1.20&#x26;width=64&#x26;height=64" alt="" data-size="line"> Tank
This kit has a slowness effect to counter its diamond armor, which allows it to tank. To be able to play, you also have an X that gives you a temporary speed effect - which counters the slowness.
