## ℹ️ How it works
This game is similar to hide and seek. At the start of the game, there is a ==seeker== whose goal is to find the other players before time runs out. The other players, the ==hiders==, can transform into blocks and objects available on the map. If a seeker manages to find and touch a player, they also become a seeker. If there are still hiders left at the end of the game, they win. If the seekers manage to find everyone, they win.

## 💸 Rewards
- 🥇 **Winner:** 50 coins 🪙
- 🥉 **Other Players:** 20 coins 🪙
